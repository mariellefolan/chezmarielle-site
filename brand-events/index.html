How it works

  1. A free 15-minute call about the date, the number of guests, your budget, and what it's for.
  2. I send you a proposal built around what you'd like me to take on.
  3. I make the mood boards and mock-ups, and I make sure you're excited about all the details and that everything looks good.
  4. On the day, I take care of everything you've handed me, so your team can focus on your guests.

Support for in-house and agency teams

Extra support for experiential marketing teams, on a contract or one-off basis, when you need help here and there but not a whole new employee. Booked by the day or by the hour.

Questions

Do we need a venue?

Nope! I can plan the dinner at your office, at a venue you already have, or find one for you.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Yes. I can join your team on a contract or one-off basis, by the day or by the hour.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many guests you're having and how much you want me to take on. Once I know what you have in mind from our 15-minute call, I'll send you a quote.

Planning something?

Send the date, the number of guests, your budget, and what it's for, and I'll get back to you.
