About

I'm Marielle, an event planner and experiential marketer in New York City.

I founded Ladies Wine Club NYC, a community for young professional women in New York. The club has more than 200 members, and I've planned and produced more than 20 of its events, from the venue and vendors to the guest list, with partners including wine bars, consumer brands, and hospitality vendors. I was also Head of Membership at Maxwell Social, a private members' club in New York City, where I planned and produced member events.
