Maxwell Social

Feast of Saint Elsa

January 2026 · 150 guests

Maxwell Social is named after Elsa Maxwell, the society hostess famous for over-the-top parties and costume balls, so for the club's twice-yearly members-only feast, we threw one in her honor. I planned the night and spent the day setting it up: I arranged all of the flowers and built the grazing spread and the champagne tower with help from the bar staff. Dinner's centerpiece was a whole roasted pig, served alongside a menu I coordinated with the club's in-house chef.
