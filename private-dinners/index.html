Who it's for

How I Can Help

I can do bundled pricing or à la carte, depending on your needs and how much you want me to take on.

  • Concept and format
  • Mood boards and mock-ups
  • Venue search, if you need one
  • Vendors and partners

  • Florals
  • Printed menus and other printed pieces
  • Spotify playlist design

How it works

  1. A free 15-minute call about the date, the number of guests, your budget, and the kind of dinner you have in mind.
  2. I send you a proposal built around what you'd like me to take on.
  3. I make the mood boards and mock-ups, and I make sure you're excited about all the details and that everything looks good.
  4. On the day, I take care of everything you've handed me, so you can enjoy your own dinner party.

Questions

Do I need a venue?

Nope! I can plan the dinner at your home, at a venue you already have, or find one for you.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many guests you're having and how much you want me to take on. Once I know what you have in mind from our 15-minute call, I'll send you a quote.

Can you take on just part of the dinner?

Of course. You can hand me just one piece, like the florals or the printed menus, or the whole dinner.

Planning a dinner?

Send the date, the number of guests, your budget, and where you'd like to host, and I'll get back to you.
